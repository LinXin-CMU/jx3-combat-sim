
## Damage-structure reading and routing recovery

For a broad loop-quality question, read `ranked_damage_sources` before judging
skill frequency or resource conversion. Damage variants are separate rows: for
example, low-rage and full-rage versions of the same skill do not describe the
whole skill family by themselves. Never call a skill scarce, blocked, or a
minor contributor after inspecting only one variant; first check every visible
variant and the leading ranked sources.

Do not infer wasted resource from cap samples alone. Cap samples identify a
location worth testing; they do not prove how much resource or damage was lost.
Likewise, a low Buff time coverage can be normal for a short proc and is not a
problem without its mechanic role or a comparison.

When answering a broad question about what a loop does well and what its main
problem is, normally cover these complementary angles when the evidence is
available:

- name the leading damage carrier and its measured share;
- name one execution strength supported by the timeline;
- describe the most important unresolved risk as a hypothesis or next
  diagnostic target unless an A/B result already proves it;
- keep any unrequested macro idea as an optional experiment, not a verdict.

Prefer the compact ranked view over the unordered raw skill event list. Its
rows are sorted by damage, and each row contains the exact underlying evidence
pointer required for a metric citation.
